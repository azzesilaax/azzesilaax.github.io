E-Commerce Usage Survey

A web page illustrating the findings of our digital commerce research

1. Demographics of Participants

The survey was conducted with a diverse group of 20 participants to understand background variations.

Criteria Data Breakdown (Total: 20 People)
Age Range 18-25: 12 people | 26-39: 5 people | 40-50: 2 people | Over 50: 1 person
Gender Female: 11 | Male: 9
Education Level High School: 2 | Some College: 10 | College Graduate: 6 | Graduate School: 2

2. Online Shopping Habits

Key metrics discovered during the interviews regarding purchasing behavior:

  • Online Purchase History: 18 participants have successfully bought items on the Web.
  • Window Shoppers: 2 participants have shopped/browsed online but never finalized a purchase.
  • Frequency of Purchases:
    • Once a week: 8 participants
    • Once a month: 7 participants
    • Once a year: 3 participants
  • Favorite Shopping Platform: Top choices include Amazon, Trendyol, and eBay.

3. Comments & Conclusions

Based on our survey results, e-commerce has become an inseparable part of modern life, especially among the 18-25 demographic where shopping frequency reaches almost a weekly routine. The ease of interface and quick access make digital stores highly preferable.

Supporting Web Statistics: This hyper-local conclusion aligns perfectly with global data. According to reports from the Pew Internet Project and eMarketer, online shopping numbers have seen massive upward shifts globally, proving that our user habits accurately reflect the grand-scale digital transformation patterns worldwide.
